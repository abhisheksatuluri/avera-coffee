Cancellation Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Because every Avera coffee is roasted fresh to order, there is a short window in which an order can be cancelled. Once roasting has begun, the beans are committed to you and cannot be returned to stock.

In short: you may cancel an order before it has been roasted and dispatched. Once it has shipped, it can no longer be cancelled.

How to Cancel

To request a cancellation, contact us as soon as possible at info@averacoffee.com or on WhatsApp at +91 90874 34124 with your order number. If your order has not yet been roasted or dispatched, we will cancel it and process a full refund.

Cancellation Window

  • Before roasting and dispatch: full cancellation and full refund.
  • After dispatch: the order cannot be cancelled. If it qualifies, you may raise a claim under our Refund and Returns Policy.

Refunds on Cancellation

Approved cancellations are refunded to your original payment method within 5 to 7 working days through Razorpay, our secure payment partner. The time for the amount to reflect depends on your bank or card provider.

Cancellations by Avera

In rare cases we may need to cancel an order, for example if an item is unavailable, if your delivery location is not serviceable, or if we are unable to verify the details of an order. In such cases we will inform you and issue a full refund.
