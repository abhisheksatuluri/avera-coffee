Policies

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Avera Specialty Coffee Roasters, operated by Aditya Food and Beverages Pvt. Ltd., respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe.

Information We Collect

We collect only what we need to serve you well:

  • Contact and delivery details: your name, email address, phone number, and shipping address, provided when you place an order, take our taste quiz, join the Avera Club, or contact us.
  • Order information: the products you buy, your preferences such as grind and size, and your order history.
  • Technical information: basic data such as your IP address, browser type, and how you use our website, gathered to keep the site secure and to improve it.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders, and to keep you updated about them.
  • To respond to your questions and provide customer support.
  • To send you updates about new roasts, offers, and the Avera Club, where you have chosen to hear from us.
  • To improve our products, website, and service.

Payments

Online payments are processed securely by Razorpay, a PCI DSS compliant payment gateway. Your card or banking details are entered directly with Razorpay and are never stored on our servers. We receive only a confirmation of payment and the details needed to fulfil your order.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with trusted partners who help us run our business, and only to the extent needed:

  • Courier and logistics partners, to deliver your order.
  • Razorpay, to process payments.
  • Service providers who help us send email and manage enquiries.

We may also disclose information where required by law.

Cookies

Our website uses a small number of cookies to keep the site working, remember your session, and understand how the site is used. You can control or disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work as intended without them.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against loss, misuse, and unauthorised access. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we work to keep your data safe.

Your Rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and you may withdraw your consent to marketing messages at any time. To make a request, write to us at info@averacoffee.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with its revision date.
