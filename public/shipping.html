Shipping Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Every Avera coffee is roasted to order, never pulled from old stock. Because freshness is the heart of what we do, your beans begin their journey only after your order is confirmed. This page explains how and when your coffee reaches you.

Roasting and Dispatch

Orders are roasted fresh and dispatched within 48 hours of your order being confirmed. During festive periods, limited releases, or high demand, dispatch may take up to 2 to 5 business days. You will always receive a tracking link by email and on WhatsApp the moment your order ships.

Delivery Timelines

  • Hyderabad and nearby areas: typically 2 to 3 working days from dispatch.
  • Other major Indian metros: typically 3 to 5 working days from dispatch.
  • Rest of India: typically 5 to 7 working days from dispatch.

Delivery timelines are estimates and may vary with location, courier load, weather, and regional conditions outside our control.

Shipping Charges

Free standard shipping on all prepaid orders above ₹350. A flat shipping fee of ₹49 applies to prepaid orders below ₹350. Shipping charges, where applicable, are shown clearly at checkout before payment.

Order Tracking

Once your order is dispatched, we send a tracking number and link by email and WhatsApp. You can follow your shipment from our roastery to your door. If your tracking has not updated for more than 48 hours, please reach out and we will investigate with the courier.

Serviceable Areas

We currently ship across India. We do not offer international shipping at this time. If your pincode is not serviceable by our courier partners, our team will contact you to arrange an alternative or to process a full refund.

Delays and Lost Shipments

If your order has not arrived within 10 days of dispatch, please contact us. We will trace the shipment with our courier partner and, if it cannot be located, send a fresh replacement or issue a full refund.
